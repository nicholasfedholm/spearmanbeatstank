The implementation list

What actually gets built once the foundation (Stage 1) passes:

  1. The Nemesis ledger — the game writes down every betrayal, sneak attack, and razed city, and AI rivals hold real grudges.
  2. Vassalage — beaten rivals can bend the knee and serve a stronger power instead of fighting to the last city. Built on top of the ledger.
  3. Loyalty — cities and vassals keep a loyalty score, with the ledger as its memory, so empires can crack from the inside.
  4. Smarter AI opponents — better war-making, diplomacy, and long-term planning.
  5. A living climate — seas that can warm and cool back down, coasts that flood, city tiles flood last, and a flooded city is lost.
  6. Multiplayer that stays in sync — two copies playing the same game turn by turn, hash-checked. Being proven right now.
  7. Speed work — measure first, then make a 2001 game run like it isn't from 2001.
